What do these words mean?
Park position
Where the ACE holds the filament after it has pulled it in by itself. Every distance is counted from here.
Hub
The junction where the tubes of all slots join into the one tube to the toolhead.
Toolhead distance
Park position to the toolhead sensor. Also used as the full retract.
Hub clearance
How far to pull back from the toolhead so the tip is out of the hub and another slot can pass.
Margin
Extra distance added on top of a measured value when saving.
